# 79. Spec: carousel (component)

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 25, 26, 39, 40, 41, 42

## Question

What is the Angular API of Yeti's `carousel` component, and what does its spec say? Its decisions are already made in the records: its row in `building-blocks.md` Part 2 (and the "Aria decisions" section where it applies), its rows in [Decide: how the package maps each of Yeti's `data-*` attributes](26-decide-yeti-data-attributes-mapping.md), its item file under [ADR 0060](../adr/0060-item-styles-are-counted-links-to-the-consumers-yeti-build.md), its ledger rows, and the shared specs it links to.

## How to work it

Write `specs/carousel.md` with the `/to-spec` template and the map's Spec shape note, from the records only. The spec decides nothing a record has not decided. Anything it needs and no record settles goes under the ticket's `### Open` for the orchestrator. Append an `## Answer` that links the spec.

### Open

Each point names where the spec carries it, its impact and confidence, and a recommendation. Points 1, 2, and 6 are HIGH impact with confidence below HIGH, so each carries a decision record (map, Standing rulings, "Trap-quadrant decisions in full AFK mode").

1. **An index `model()` or a read-only `current` signal** (spec sections 2 and 4; HIGH impact, MEDIUM confidence; trap quadrant).
   - **Question.** Building-blocks 1.4, architecture-guide P10, and the events spec's rule 8 list "the carousel's index" among the states that get a `model()`. Part 2 row 29, the carousel's own row, gives the root "a `current` signal from an `IntersectionObserver`" and no model. Which is the public API?
   - **Option A (recommended): a read-only `current: Signal<number | null>` and the `slide` output, no model.** Approved because 1.4's own criterion is "state Yeti exposes as both an attribute and an event", and the carousel exposes no attribute for its index: nothing in its manifest holds it. Row 29 is the specific record and names a signal. A model would need its write path specified: a parent write would have to scroll the track from an `afterRenderEffect`, decide whether a write emits `indexChange`, and decide what the model holds while a swipe is in flight. No record specifies any of that.
   - **Option B: a `model<number>()` (for example `index`) beside `slide`.** Dismissed for now: it doubles `slide` on the choice (`indexChange` and `slide` would fire together), and its write path is new behaviour no record decides. It would give a consumer a way to drive the carousel from state, which option A leaves to the dots and the buttons.
   - **Option C: a public `scrollTo(index)` method instead of a model.** Dismissed: no record names it, and Material's vocabulary (building-blocks 1.3) has no such verb.
   - **Evidence and confidence.** Read: 1.4's criterion; row 29; the manifest (no index attribute); events rule 8. Not measured. MEDIUM, because three general records name a model and one specific record does not.
   - **To overrule.** For B: add the model to section 4's table, specify that a parent write scrolls the track in an `afterRenderEffect` write phase with the dot's formula and emits nothing (events rule 9), add a layer-2 case for the write, and keep `current` as the observed arrival. For C: add the method and its layer-2 case. Either way, correct building-blocks 1.4 and events rule 8 to match whichever wins. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 121 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

2. **Out-of-view slides once the application is live** (spec section 7; HIGH impact, MEDIUM confidence; trap quadrant).
   - **Question.** ADR 0024 point 1 says: "If the `carousel` spec makes slides out of view unreachable once live (the APG's 'hidden, not off-screen' rule ...), it applies that only from the carousel's first render callback". No record says whether the spec does. Does the package add `inert` to slides out of view after hydration?
   - **Option A (recommended): no; every slide stays reachable in every mode.** Approved because no record adds it (Part 2 row 29 and A11Y-4 list what the package adds, and `inert` is not among them); one behaviour in every mode is simpler to test and to explain; `inert` content cannot be reached by find-in-page or by Tab, which would hide a slide's links from a keyboard user who has not scrolled to it; and with `slides` above 1, deciding which partly visible slide is "out of view" is a threshold no record sets. The APG's concern (`carousel-pattern.html:30`) is slides "incorrectly hidden, e.g., displayed off-screen" in a rotating carousel; Yeti's slides are side by side in a scroller, and a screen reader's reading cursor scrolls the one it reaches into view (inferred).
   - **Option B: `inert` on every slide the observer reports less than half in view, from the first render callback.** Dismissed for now: it adds a state attribute the package writes after hydration on consumer elements, makes Tab skip controls on other slides, and changes what assistive technology reaches after a swipe in a way no measurement covers.
   - **Evidence and confidence.** Read: ADR 0024, A11Y-4, the APG text. Not measured with a screen reader. MEDIUM.
   - **To overrule.** For B: bind `[attr.inert]` on `YetiCarouselSlide` from the observer's set, `null` until the first callback (so the server HTML and the hydration pass stay free of it, as ADR 0024 requires), add a layer-1 case that a swipe makes the new slide reachable, add a layer-4 Tab walk, and amend A11Y-4's "What the package adds". Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 122 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

3. **What "the slide in view" is, and when `aria-current` first renders** (spec sections 4, 7, 8, and 10; MEDIUM impact, MEDIUM confidence). Recommendation: `current` is the first slide in DOM order that the observer reports at least half inside the track (the start-edge slide under `scroll-snap-align: start`, and the first of several when `slides` is above 1), and `null` until the first callback. So no dot carries `aria-current` in the server HTML, with JavaScript off, before hydration, or inside `hydrate never`. Reason: Yeti's own position is that without script "they cannot report which slide is showing"; a server-rendered mark on the first dot was measured to stay on dot 1 for good inside `hydrate never` ([aria-carousel findings](../prototypes/aria-carousel/README.md) point 3c), which states something false once the reader scrolls. Alternative: render `aria-current` on the first dot on the server, as prototype (A) did. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 123 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

4. **Previous and next** (spec sections 2, 4, 7, and 10; MEDIUM impact, MEDIUM confidence). Recommendation, as the spec reads: no wrap-around; `aria-disabled="true"` while there is no slide that way, and while `current()` is `null`, which includes the server HTML, JavaScript off, before hydration, and `hydrate never`; the step starts from the last choice still being scrolled to, else from the start-edge slide measured at click time; both emit `slide`; neither moves focus. Reasons: the carousel does not rotate, so wrapping has no APG basis; `aria-disabled` keeps the focused button in the Tab sequence (as the APG prefers for pickers, `:182-183`) and Yeti's button already styles it; a button that looks ready and does nothing with JavaScript off is worse than one marked unavailable, and the buttons cannot be left out of the server HTML (no platform branch); `slide` on every choice is what `carousel.js:38-41` says the index is for ("a counter or a caption beside the carousel"). Alternatives: wrap-around; buttons enabled in the server HTML as a stated residue (prototype (A)'s state); `hidden` until live (a layout shift at hydration); no `slide` from the buttons (`yeti:slide` fired only for dots). Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 124 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

5. **The dot handler's `defaultPrevented` check, and a dot clicked before hydration** (spec sections 2, 4, and 10; MEDIUM impact, MEDIUM confidence). `carousel.js:16` leaves a prevented click to the browser. Angular's dispatcher calls `preventDefault()` on a click whose action element is an `<a>` with `jsaction` while it dispatches it (`packages/core/primitives/event-dispatch/src/dispatcher.ts:83-85`, `:127-140`, read). So inside a dehydrated `@defer (hydrate on ...)` block the native navigation is cancelled and the click is replayed to the handler with `defaultPrevented` already true; with the check, that click would be lost. Recommendation: do not check `defaultPrevented` (a change from `carousel.js`, stated in the Module-replaced table), keep the modifier and button checks, and call `preventDefault()` last. A consumer's template `(click)` on the dot runs after the directive's host listener anyway, so the check could not serve as its veto either (ticket 73's reading for the alert). Related: the events spec says a replayed dot click "reaches the package handler once the boundary hydrates, and the handler then emits: late, but once", while ticket 29's prototype measured no `slide` for a click before full hydration ("cause not pinned"); calling `preventDefault()` first (as `carousel.js:25` orders it) throws during replay and skips the emit, which is one inferred cause. Layer 4 measures both cases. Alternative: keep the check like-for-like and state both losses as residues. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 125 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

6. **Data-driven dots** (spec section 4, "Data-driven dots", and usage rule 4; HIGH impact, MEDIUM confidence; trap quadrant).
   - **Question.** Ticket 50 decision 4 has the dot read the consumer's static `href` once through `HostAttributeToken('href')`. Inside `@for`, a static `href` cannot carry each slide's id, and `href="#{{ id }}"` is a binding that `HostAttributeToken` does not see and that competes with the directive's `[attr.href]`. Where does a data-driven dot's fragment come from?
   - **Option A (recommended): a selector-named input `yetiCarouselDot: string | undefined`, the slide's id, passed to `injectSameDocumentHref` as a signal; when set, the consumer writes no `href`; `HostAttributeToken('href')` is injected optionally.** Approved because the fragment-links spec already accepts "a string or a signal of one, so a part whose fragment can change stays current" and leaves the source to the owning spec (its section 4 and usage rule 2), the static form of decision 4 still works unchanged, and the selector-named form follows the user's choice for selector-named inputs ("Selector name (Recommended)", map, Standing rulings).
   - **Option B: static `href` only; a data-driven carousel writes its dots by hand.** Dismissed: most Angular carousels are rendered from data, and `@for` is the documented way to do it.
   - **Option C: the dot finds its slide by position (the n-th dot is the n-th slide) and builds the fragment from the slide's `id`.** Dismissed: it couples dots to slides by order, which no record sets and which breaks when the two lists differ, and Yeti's contract is the link's own target.
   - **Evidence and confidence.** Read: decision 4, fragment-links section 4, `HostAttributeToken`'s static-only reading (inferred from Angular's API, not run). MEDIUM, because the input is new API on a part.
   - **To overrule.** For B: remove the input from section 4's table, usage rule 4, the `carousel--dynamic` story, and the layer-2 case, and add a usage rule that dots are written statically. For C: replace the input with order-based matching and specify what happens when the counts differ. The toc spec (ticket 91) faces the same question for its links and should match whichever wins. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 126 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

7. **The track's `role="group"` and `tabindex="0"`** (spec sections 2 and 4, usage rule 2; MEDIUM impact, MEDIUM confidence). Part 2 row 29 gives the track only "static `data-track`"; Yeti's manifest asks the author for both attributes. Recommendation: static host attributes on `YetiCarouselTrack`, as Part 2 row 14 does for the scroller's `role="region"` and `tabindex="0"`, so a consumer cannot forget the focus stop (`scrollable-region-focusable` in the Story gate). Alternative: leave both to the consumer as Yeti's markup does, with a usage rule. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 127 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

8. **`aria-live` on the track** (spec section 7; LOW impact, MEDIUM confidence). Part 2 row 29 calls it "the spec's call". Recommendation: not bound. Scrolling a snap track changes no DOM, so a live region on it has nothing to announce (inferred); the APG makes it optional (`:147-150`), and there is no rotation. A consumer who wants an announcement writes its own live text beside the carousel from `current`. Alternative: bind `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-atomic="false"` as the APG's optional markup, measured with a screen reader as a manual release check. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 128 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

9. **Slide hosts** (spec usage rule 3; MEDIUM impact, HIGH confidence). Binding `role="group"` on Yeti's `article` slides fails axe's `aria-allowed-role` (`best-practice`, which the Story gate runs; measured in ticket 29's prototype (A)). Recommendation: a usage rule that a slide is a `div`, `section`, `figure`, or `blockquote`, never an `article` or an `li` (ARIA in HTML allows `group` on the first four and not on the last two, read), plus an **Anti-pattern story** for the `article` form. Alternative: bind no role on an `article` host, which leaves A11Y-4's slide role unmet there. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 129 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

10. **`aria-roledescription="slide"` as a package string** (spec sections 2, 4, and 10, usage rule 11; MEDIUM impact, MEDIUM confidence). Part 2 row 29, A11Y-4, and building-blocks 1.10's deviations bullet bind it; 1.10's Strings bullet says every "role description" comes from consumer markup. Recommendation: keep it as a static host attribute, and document that a consumer's static `aria-roledescription` on the slide (with `i18n-aria-roledescription`) replaces it, because Angular merges template attributes after directive host attributes (`NGP/core/src/render3/view/elements.ts:54-55`, read by the scroller spec, not run; layer 2 asserts it). Alternative: leave the attribute to the consumer, as the root's `aria-roledescription="carousel"` is, with a usage rule. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 130 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

11. **Forced colours** (spec section 7; MEDIUM impact, LOW confidence). The dot is a `::before` circle painted with `background-color`, which forced-colours mode may replace with the canvas colour (inferred, not measured). Recommendation, after ticket 50 decision 88's pattern: no ledger row and no package CSS now; layer 4 asserts the dots stay visible under `forcedColors: 'active'` in three engines; if they do not, a ledger row owned by `carousel` and one `@layer ngx-yeti` rule follow. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 131 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

12. **WebKit's Tab and the dots** (spec section 7; LOW impact, MEDIUM confidence). WebKit skips links on Tab by default ([aria-carousel findings](../prototypes/aria-carousel/README.md), measured). Recommendation: add no `tabindex` to the dots, which would override the reader's Safari preference; previous, next, and the focusable track are the keyboard path there, and A11Y-4 says so. Alternative: a static `tabindex="0"` on each dot (ticket 29's B variant shows WebKit's Tab reaches a link with a `tabindex`). Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 132 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

13. **`aria-controls` on previous and next** (spec section 3; LOW impact, MEDIUM confidence). The APG's pattern text does not require it (read, `carousel-pattern.html:117-150`). Recommendation: none, so the carousel needs no generated id. Alternative: generate an id for the track through [generated-ids](../specs/generated-ids.md) and bind `aria-controls` on both buttons. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 133 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

14. **Ledger A11Y-4's cells** (spec section 7; LOW impact, HIGH confidence). Recommendation for the orchestrator, who edits the ledger: "What the package adds" gains "no `aria-live` on the track (inferred to announce nothing); `aria-current` absent until the observer's first report; previous and next `aria-disabled` at the ends and until live; previous, next, and the track are the keyboard path in WebKit, whose Tab skips the dot links (ticket 29, measured)". "Tested by" gains L2 (the handler and the observer) and L3 (no `inert` or `hidden`, no `aria-current`, `aria-disabled` in the server HTML). Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 134 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).

15. **Record wording that reads against the spec** (LOW impact, HIGH confidence). For the orchestrator:
    - ADR 0023's 2026-10-03 note and ticket 50 decision 4 say the directive "never binds `href`", while the fragment-links spec (section 4) and this spec bind `[attr.href]` from `injectSameDocumentHref` after reading the static `href` once. Ticket 41's own open point 1 describes the binding. The wording should say "never binds the consumer's `href` as state; it binds the same-document form".
    - `architecture-guide.md` writes `div[yetiCarousel]` (row 29: `section[yetiCarousel]`), lists `FocusKeyManager`, `Directionality`, and `LiveAnnouncer` as preferred for the carousel (row 29 rejects all three), and prefers "`Directionality.valueSignal()` for the carousel's arrow keys" (the carousel has no arrow-key handler; the arrows scroll the focused track natively). The guide ranks below the records (map, Precedence), so the spec follows row 29. Decided 2026-10-03 in ticket 50, decision 135 (orchestrator, full AFK mode).
