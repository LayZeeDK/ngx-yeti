# Intent — ngx-yeti M002 layouts-and-button

Lane: milestone

Lane reason: derived from the approved ROADMAP.md entry M002, which lists no open questions, so planning follows directly.

Review panel: off

Finding skeptics: off

Surfaces: Storybook stories, Fixture app routes, package entry points

## Summary

All 17 layouts plus `table`, `visually-hidden`, `enter`, `button`, and `spinner` ship at their own entry points with their ledger rows, giving every later item its composition partners. This is the second milestone of the program in `.project/CHARTER.md` (ROADMAP.md M002). It builds on the M001 walking skeleton: the ADR 0060 style loader, the secondary entry-point layout, the contract check, and the packed consuming build. It also lands the cases M001 carried over because they compose these items. Source: ROADMAP.md M002 Goal; program decisions in `.project/SYNTHESIS.md`.

## Problem

After M001 the package has two items (`card`, `lift`) and the style loader. Most of Yeti's remaining items compose layouts (box, stack, center, cluster, grid, and others) or `button`, so no later milestone can ship its stories, Fixture routes, or composite cases until these 22 items exist. The setup spec's order-sensitive pairs and several M001 cases also wait on them.

## Users

- Angular 22.2 application developers who consume `ngx-yeti` to use Yeti (Foundation 7) layouts and buttons with typed directives, SSR, hydration, and JavaScript-off rendering.
- The package maintainer (the user), who publishes the package and relies on the four test layers and CI to prove each item.
- Pipeline coders and reviewers in M003 to M009, who compose these items in later stories, Fixture routes, and Examples.
