# T016 — Ship spinner with A11Y-13 and its forced-colours measurement

## Context

`spinner` (docs/specs/specs/spinner.md; `YetiSpinner`) is a named status region with a turning ring. It owns ledger row A11Y-13 (the animation is essential; one iteration under reduced motion, asserted at layer 4). spinner.md:215 measures the ring under forced colours and expects the bright edge may be lost; if so, a ledger row owned by spinner and one `@layer ngx-yeti` rule follow (ticket 50 decision 193). Button acquires `spinner` too (button.md:332): the cases that need a `yetiButton` (spinner.md:304, :311, :330) land with button (T020). The phrase-capture measurement of spinner.md:197 is SC9's (T027).
