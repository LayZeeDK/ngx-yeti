# T012 — Ship cluster with its enter shared host, the lift carry-over, and timeline's cluster measurement

## Context

`cluster` (docs/specs/specs/cluster.md; `YetiCluster`) is the wrapping row many later stories sit in (button, icon, container, lift). Its layer-2 and layer-4 cases compose `yetiEnter` (T011), and its `align` is a removed-kind static attribute measured by T003. It also lands the M001 carry-over `lift--default` with its three cards in a `yetiCluster` (lift.md:224) and the timeline spec's measurement of a timeline inside a cluster (timeline.md:316, ticket 50 decision 66), recorded per engine. Cluster's list semantics under a screen reader (cluster.md:69) are SC9's (T027); `cluster--legend`'s `yetiNumeric` value lands with table (T023).
