## Finding

- **Found**: on the Storybook stories surface (the static build at PLAN.md's Entry), no inline `color-scheme` wrapper keeps its own scheme. Both wrappers of a story always render the page media's scheme: measured light `oklch(0.99 0.02 250)` / `oklch(0.15 0.02 250)` inside "dark" wrappers under default media. The cause is Vite's default `build.cssTarget` (`baseline-widely-available`: chrome111, firefox114, safari16.4). Vite passes it to the lightningcss minify in `build`, and lightningcss lowers `light-dark()` into `prefers-color-scheme`-keyed `--lightningcss-*` variables that an inline `color-scheme` cannot switch. The dev transform keeps native `light-dark()`, so the play functions and `nx storybook` show the correct dark colours. In the static build, ngx-yeti-e2e's dark-scheme contrast checks at button.spec.ts:244, layer.spec.ts:52, overlay.spec.ts:213, and spinner.spec.ts:287/:320 measure, record, and pass on light colours. Their titles and annotations still claim the dark scheme. So the e2e layer gives no evidence for the dark halves of button.md:315, spinner.md:290/:292, overlay.md:291, and ledger A11Y-10b.
- **Fix direction**: in packages/ngx-yeti/.storybook/main.ts `viteFinal`, set `build.cssTarget` to targets derived from .browserslistrc in the returned `mergeConfig`. Take them from the installed `browserslist` (or lightningcss `browserslistToTargets`), converted to Vite's esbuild-style list, e.g. `chrome141, edge141, firefox145, safari26.2, ios26.2`. lightningcss then keeps `light-dark()` native, and no story changes. Do not set `css.lightningcss.targets`: Vite's `minifyCSS` overrides it (node.js:30543). Proof:

  2. Re-run the wrapper probe and confirm the dark wrappers compute `oklch(0.13 0.02 250)` under page media light.
